## 🗺️ Features Overview

### 1. **Location Zones Management** (लोकेशन ज़ोन)

Create and manage different zones on your sitemap with custom pricing.

#### How to Create a Zone:

1. Click **"लोकेशन ज़ोन"** tab
2. Click **"नया ज़ोन बनाएं"** button
3. Fill in details:
   - **Zone Name:** e.g., "मुख्य मार्ग के पास"
   - **Min Price:** e.g., 12500 (per sq yd)
   - **Max Price:** e.g., 15000 (per sq yd)
4. Click **"कोऑर्डिनेट्स पिक करें"**
5. Click on sitemap to create polygon points
6. Click **"Save Coordinates"**
7. Click **"ज़ोन सेव करें"**

#### Zone Features:
- ✅ Custom pricing for different locations
- ✅ Visual polygon on sitemap
- ✅ Color-coded zones
- ✅ Edit/Delete zones anytime

---

### 2. **Plot Management** (प्लॉट्स मैनेज करें)

Add and manage individual plots with precise coordinates.

#### How to Add a Plot:

1. Click **"प्लॉट्स मैनेज करें"** tab
2. Click **"नया प्लॉट जोड़ें"** button
3. Fill in details:
   - **Plot Number:** e.g., "101" or "A-25"
   - **Area:** e.g., "75.5" (supports decimals)
   - **Price:** e.g., "950000"
   - **Status:** Available/Sold/Booked
   - **Owner Name:** (if sold/booked)
4. Click **"कोऑर्डिनेट्स पिक करें"**
5. Click on sitemap to mark plot location
6. Click **"Save Coordinates"**
7. Click **"प्लॉट सेव करें"**

#### Plot Features:
- ✅ Decimal area support (e.g., 75.5, 100.25)
- ✅ Visual coordinate picker
- ✅ Status management (Available/Sold/Booked)
- ✅ Owner name for sold plots
- ✅ Edit/Delete plots anytime
- ✅ Real-time updates on website

#### Plot Status Colors:
- 🟢 **Green:** Available
- 🔴 **Red:** Sold
- 🟡 **Yellow:** Booked

## 🎨 Coordinate Picker Guide

The coordinate picker helps you mark exact locations on the sitemap.

### For Zones (Polygon):
1. Click multiple points to create a polygon
2. Minimum 3 points required
3. Points connect automatically
4. Last point connects to first point
5. Click "Save Coordinates" when done

### For Plots (Single Point):
1. Click once on the plot location
2. A marker appears at that point
3. Click "Save Coordinates" to confirm

### Tips:
- 🔍 Zoom in for precise placement
- 🖱️ Click carefully on plot boundaries
- ↩️ Use "Clear" to start over
- 💾 Always save before closing

## 📊 Best Practices

### Zone Management:
1. **Create zones first** before adding plots
2. **Use descriptive names** (e.g., "Highway Facing", "Park View")
3. **Set realistic price ranges** based on location
4. **Don't overlap zones** - keep boundaries clear

### Plot Management:
1. **Add plots only after customer booking**
2. **Use consistent numbering** (e.g., A-1, A-2, B-1, B-2)
3. **Enter exact area** from measurement
4. **Add owner name** immediately after sale
5. **Update status** promptly (Available → Booked → Sold)

### Inquiry Management:
1. **Check daily** for new inquiries
2. **Respond within 24 hours**
3. **Mark as contacted** after first call
4. **Mark as converted** after booking
5. **Keep notes** of customer preferences

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Can't Login?
- ✅ Check email spelling
- ✅ Verify password (case-sensitive)
- ✅ Clear browser cache
- ✅ Try different browser

### Plot Not Showing on Website?
- ✅ Check if coordinates are saved
- ✅ Verify plot status is correct
- ✅ Refresh website (Ctrl+F5)
- ✅ Check browser console for errors

### Coordinates Not Saving?
- ✅ Click on sitemap image area
- ✅ Wait for marker to appear
- ✅ Click "Save Coordinates" button
- ✅ Don't close modal before saving

### Inquiries Not Loading?
- ✅ Check internet connection
- ✅ Refresh page
- ✅ Check Firebase connection
- ✅ Verify Firestore rules
